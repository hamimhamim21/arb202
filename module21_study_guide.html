كَوَى - يَكْوِي - اِكْوِ

To Iron (clothes)

تَصْرِيفُ الفِعْلِ Verb Conjugation

About This Verb - عن هذا الفعل

كَوَى (kawaa) means "to iron (clothes)". This is a defective verb (فعل ناقص) where the final letter is a weak letter (ى/ي).

The three forms:

  • كَوَى (Past/الماضي) - He ironed
  • يَكْوِي (Present/المضارع) - He irons / is ironing
  • اِكْوِ (Command/الأمر) - Iron! (command to male)

Related word: مِكْوَاة (mikwaah) = iron (the device)

الأَعْدَادُ المُرَكَّبَة Compound Numbers (11-19)

What Are Compound Numbers? - ما هي الأعداد المركبة؟

Compound numbers in Arabic are numbers from 11 to 19. They are called "compound" because they combine two parts: a unit (1-9) + ten (عَشَر/عَشْرَة).

Example: ثَلَاثَةَ عَشَرَ = three + ten = thirteen (13)

The Key Rule - القاعدة الأساسية

The pronunciation of "ten" (عشر) changes based on whether the counted noun is masculine or feminine:

  • For MASCULINE nouns: Use عَشَرَ (with fatha on ش - 'ashara)
  • For FEMININE nouns: Use عَشْرَة (with sukoon on ش - 'ashrata)

Memory tip: The ش (sheen) has an OPEN vowel (fatha) for masculine, and is CLOSED (sukoon) for feminine.

Important Grammar Notes - ملاحظات نحوية مهمة

  • The counted noun (المعدود) is always singular and mansoob (منصوب) with tanween fatha (ـاً)
  • Example: أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كِتَاباً (eleven books) - not كُتُب
  • Numbers 11 and 12 have special forms that match the gender of the counted noun
  • Numbers 13-19 follow the "opposite gender" rule for the unit part

وَزْنُ فَعْلان / فَعْلَى Adjective Pattern for Physical/Emotional States

What is the فَعْلان Pattern? - ما هو وزن فَعْلان؟

The فَعْلان (fa'laan) pattern is used for adjectives that describe temporary physical or emotional states like hunger, thirst, anger, etc.

Key features:

  • Masculine singular: فَعْلان (fa'laan) - ends with ان
  • Feminine singular: فَعْلَى (fa'laa) - ends with ى (NOT ة!)
  • Plural (both genders): Usually فِعَال (fi'aal) - e.g., جِيَاع, عِطَاش

Why is this pattern important? - لماذا هذا الوزن مهم؟

Unlike regular adjectives that form feminine with ة (taa marboota), these adjectives use ى (alif maqsoora) for feminine:

  • Regular adjective: كَبِير → كَبِيرَة (big)
  • فَعْلان adjective: جَوْعَان → جَوْعَى (hungry) - NOT جَوْعَانَة!

أَظُنُّ أَنَّ Expressing Opinion: "I think that..."

What is أَظُنُّ أَنَّ? - ما هو أَظُنُّ أَنَّ؟

أَظُنُّ أَنَّ (adhunnu anna) means "I think that..." or "I believe that..."

It is used to express an opinion or belief that you are not 100% certain about.

Grammar Structure - البنية النحوية

  • أَظُنُّ = I think (present tense verb from ظَنَّ - يَظُنُّ)
  • أَنَّ = that (conjunction that introduces a noun clause)
  • After أَنَّ, use a noun or pronoun suffix (اسم أنَّ)

Pattern: أَظُنُّ + أَنَّ + (noun/pronoun) + (predicate)

Examples with Different Pronouns - أمثلة مع الضمائر

أَظُنُّ أَنَّكَ طَبِيبٌ

I think (that) you (m) are a doctor

أَظُنُّ أَنَّكِ مُعَلِّمَةٌ

I think (that) you (f) are a teacher

أَظُنُّ أَنَّهُ مِنَ اليَابَانِ

I think (that) he is from Japan

أَظُنُّ أَنَّهَا مَرِيضَةٌ

I think (that) she is sick

أَظُنُّ أَنَّ الوَلَدَ نَائِمٌ

I think (that) the boy is sleeping

مُلَخَّصُ الدَّرْس - Lesson Summary

  • Main Verb: كَوَى - يَكْوِي - اِكْوِ = To iron (clothes)
  • Compound Numbers (11-19): Use عَشَرَ (fatha on ش) with masculine nouns, عَشْرَة (sukoon on ش) with feminine nouns
  • فَعْلان/فَعْلَى Pattern: Adjectives for temporary states - masculine ends in ان, feminine ends in ى (not ة)
  • Examples: جَوْعَان/جَوْعَى (hungry), عَطْشَان/عَطْشَى (thirsty), غَضْبَان/غَضْبَى (angry)
  • Plurals: فِعَال pattern - جِيَاع (hungry people), عِطَاش (thirsty people)
  • أَظُنُّ أَنَّ: "I think that..." - used to express opinions (أَظُنُّ أَنَّكَ = I think you are...)
